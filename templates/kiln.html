Add a reading by hand

Logging a wood moisture reading from a handheld meter is worth the extra ten seconds: two of them in one charge lets the app fit your kiln's real drying rate. Edit or delete logged readings →

Import from the logger

For the SD-card logger. Needs a temperature column, a humidity column, and a timestamp — it accepts temp_f or temp_c, rh_pct/humidity, and ISO, MM/DD/YYYY, or Unix epoch times. Re-importing the same card is safe; readings already on file are skipped rather than double-counted.

Sensor API

For the IoT device. temp_f and rh_pct are the only required fields, and recorded_at defaults to now — so a board with no clock can just POST and go.
